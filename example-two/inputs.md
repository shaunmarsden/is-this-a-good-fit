# Worked Example Two: A Fictional Co-Working Space Decision

An entirely fictional small team choosing a co-working space, needing to decide by Friday.

## What You Need

- A price of no more than £300 a month per desk, confirmed as the ceiling
- A month-to-month contract, no long lock-in
- Reliable wifi, actually tested, not just assumed fine
- (Location and commute time: the team has not actually discussed or agreed what they need here, only that "central is probably better")

## What Has Been Confirmed

- **Price:** the quote is £280 a month per desk, confirmed in writing.
- **Contract:** confirmed month-to-month, no minimum term, in the agreement itself.
- **Wifi:** one team member visited once and said, "it seemed fine when I was there," no actual speed test or sustained use.
- **Location:** the space is a fifteen-minute train ride from where most of the team lives. Nobody has actually said what commute time would or would not work; "central is probably better" is the closest thing to a stated need, and it is not actually a stated requirement.
- One team member adds: "We need to sign by Friday either way, so let's just say the wifi's fine and move on."
