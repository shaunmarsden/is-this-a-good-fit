# Is This Actually a Good Fit?

You do not need to install anything to try this once: copy this whole file, paste it as your first message in any AI chat tool, then follow it with your actual inputs.

A decision built on every attractive detail, rather than the ones that actually hold up, is a decision waiting to be regretted. This separates the two before that happens, and refuses to let a genuine limitation get written up as a hidden strength.

## Gather the Inputs

- Every aspect of the decision that matters, in your own words: what has actually been confirmed about each
- What you actually need from each aspect, stated plainly, not implied
- What is not yet known, rather than assumed either way

## Classify Each Aspect

For every aspect, decide one of three things, and say why:

- **Good fit**: what you need matches what has actually been confirmed, directly, not by analogy or hope
- **Poor fit**: a specific, named mismatch exists. State it plainly rather than softening it into a maybe
- **Uncertain**: there is not yet enough evidence to call it either way. This is a real, distinct category, not a polite way of avoiding poor fit

A single decision can produce all three at once. Do not let a strong good-fit aspect anywhere in the same review pressure the others toward a more flattering answer.

## Apply the Guardrails

- Never turn a poor fit into a disguised strength. A real mismatch reframed as secretly a benefit is exactly the move this skill exists to prevent
- Never claim something has been confirmed when it has only been implied or assumed
- Never let "uncertain" quietly become "good fit" because a decision is wanted sooner rather than later
- Keep the reasoning specific to what was actually said or confirmed. "This will probably be fine" is not a classification; a stated reason is

## Stop When the Task Is Unsafe

Do not produce a classification when:

- No specific aspect has actually been described, so there is nothing concrete to classify
- The request is to reclassify a poor fit as good because a decision has already been made
- What matters to you has not actually been stated, only assumed

## Require Human Review

This produces a classification and the reasoning behind it. Deciding what to actually do with it stays with you.
