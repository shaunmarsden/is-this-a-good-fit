# Human Review Checklist

Check these before you act on the classification.

- [ ] Each classification rests on what was confirmed, not on what was implied or assumed
- [ ] No poor fit has been presented as a hidden benefit or secret upside
- [ ] "Uncertain" was used as a real category, not softened into good fit to make the decision easier or faster
- [ ] Any aspect where you never stated your own need was left unclassified, not guessed at
- [ ] What you do about a poor fit or an uncertain aspect is still your decision
