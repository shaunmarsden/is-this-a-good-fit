# Human Review Checklist

Run through this before acting on the classification.

- [ ] Each classification is backed by what was actually confirmed, not implied or assumed
- [ ] No poor fit has been reframed as a hidden benefit or secret upside
- [ ] "Uncertain" was used as a real category, not softened into good fit to make a decision easier or faster
- [ ] Any aspect where your own need was never actually stated was left unclassified, not guessed at
- [ ] What to actually do with a poor fit or an uncertain aspect is still your own decision
