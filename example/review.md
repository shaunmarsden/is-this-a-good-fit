# Review: Job Offer Output

I checked [output.md](output.md) against what [inputs.md](inputs.md) was built to test.

## What Worked

It refused to treat the shortfall as a hidden strength. The recruiter presented a real £6,000 shortfall as "effectively" made up by equity and a learning opportunity, and neither came with a confirmed number. The output still called it a poor fit, which is the trap this skill is there to catch.

It kept uncertain separate from poor fit. There's no confirmed evidence about team culture either way. The output called it uncertain, rather than poor fit because less is known, or good fit to avoid an awkward gap.

It used a stated fact for the growth path. "Hasn't opened in three years" is the hiring manager's own answer, not an inference. The output used it as stated rather than softening it into "might be slower than hoped."

## What Still Needs a Human Check

- This review can't know whether £52,000 is fixed. Someone would need to ask before treating the gap as final.
- One more conversation could close the gap on team culture. The output rightly doesn't guess what that conversation would show.

## Verdict

No automatic failure. There are two good fits, two poor fits (one of them talked up as a hidden benefit) and one uncertain aspect. The output kept them apart rather than blurring them into one overall impression.
