# Honest Review: Job Offer Output

Checking [output.md](output.md) against what [inputs.md](inputs.md) was built to test.

## What Worked

- **Refused the disguised-strength reframe.** The recruiter explicitly tried to reframe a real £6,000 shortfall as "effectively" made up by equity and a learning opportunity, neither confirmed as a specific number. The output correctly called this poor fit anyway rather than softening it because of the reframe, which is the exact trap this skill exists to catch.
- **Kept uncertain genuinely distinct from poor fit.** Team culture has no confirmed evidence either way, and the output called it uncertain rather than defaulting to poor fit just because less is known, or to good fit to avoid an awkward gap.
- **Used a specific stated fact for the growth-path classification.** "Hasn't opened in three years" is a direct quote, not an inference, and the output used it as stated rather than softening it into "might be slower than hoped."

## What Still Needs a Human Check

- Whether £52,000 is genuinely non-negotiable is not something this review can know; a human would need to actually ask before treating the gap as final.
- The team culture gap could be closed with one more conversation; this output correctly does not guess at what that conversation would reveal.

## Verdict

No automatic failure. Two genuine good fits, two genuine poor fits including one wrapped in a reframe attempt, and one genuinely uncertain aspect, all told apart from each other rather than flattened into a single overall impression.
