# Fit Review: Fictional Job Offer

Source: [inputs.md](inputs.md).

## Role Scope

**Good fit.** Confirmed hands-on technical work, matching what was stated as needed directly, not by inference.

## Compensation

**Poor fit.** The confirmed salary, £52,000, is £6,000 below the stated minimum of £58,000. The recruiter's equity and "learning opportunity" framing does not change this: neither was offered as a specific, confirmed number, and reframing a real shortfall as secretly a bigger opportunity is exactly the move to refuse here. This stays a poor fit unless the actual number changes.

## Remote Flexibility

**Good fit.** Confirmed fully remote in the offer letter itself, matching the stated need directly.

## Team Culture

**Uncertain.** Only one call has happened, with the hiring manager specifically, not the team. Nothing about day-to-day dynamics, communication style, or pace has actually been confirmed either way. This is not a soft "probably fine", it is a genuine gap.

## Career Growth Path

**Poor fit.** Asked directly, the hiring manager confirmed the next title up has not opened in three years. This is a specific, stated fact, not an assumption, and it directly contradicts the stated need for a visible path within two years.

## Summary

Two aspects are a good fit, two are a poor fit, and one is genuinely uncertain rather than either. The compensation gap in particular came with an attempt to reframe it as a hidden benefit, which does not change what was actually confirmed: a real shortfall against a stated minimum.
