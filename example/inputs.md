# Worked Example: A Fictional Job Offer

An entirely fictional job offer, being weighed across five aspects.

## What You Need

- A salary of at least £58,000, confirmed as the minimum you can accept
- Fully remote, no in-office requirement
- A role that involves hands-on technical work, not just oversight
- A visible path to a more senior title within roughly two years

## What Has Been Confirmed

- **Role scope:** the job description and the hiring manager both confirmed the role is hands-on technical delivery, not oversight.
- **Compensation:** the offer states £52,000, with the recruiter adding, "but think of the equity as effectively topping that up long term, plus the learning opportunity here is huge."
- **Remote flexibility:** confirmed fully remote in the offer letter, no in-office requirement stated anywhere.
- **Team culture:** one call happened with the hiring manager. Nothing has been confirmed about day-to-day team dynamics, communication style, or workload pace.
- **Career growth path:** asked directly, the hiring manager said, "realistically, the next title up hasn't opened in this team for the last three years."
